# LoopLab product context

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

LoopLab's studio website presents its software services, projects, working process, and product packages. Visitors can explore project details and begin a contextual enquiry through the existing contact form.

## Operating Context

This record describes the local testing checkout. The implementation uses React, React Router, Vite, and Tailwind with shared CSS. It does not establish a production deployment or verify live contact delivery.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The homepage, project gallery, Services, Studio, Packages, Careers, and Insights have refined interiors. Contact uses the shared visual style. Account flows and existing service integrations are preserved; Careers provides direct email applications while the jobs feed is unavailable.
- Projects support category filtering and expandable descriptions. Preserve the recorded status of each project, including the sales incentive platform being in development.
- Packages support category filtering and monthly or full-project preferences. Enquiry links prefill the contact subject; a preference is not a purchase or agreed commercial term.
- Prices, commercial terms, and delivery commitments remain open decisions. Do not invent prices, testimonials, customer claims, metrics, or guarantees.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the LoopLab name, original logo lettering and orbital identity, purple and blue palette, Space Grotesk headings, and Manrope body text. Exact visual values live in DESIGN.md. The refined transparent logo appears in the header. The approved sculptural loop occupies the hero visual field once, with its full aspect ratio preserved and no overlap over text or controls. The approved Fidelity bust image is illustrative project artwork.

## Evidence on Hand

The original logo is retained at `src/assets/logo1.webp`. User-supplied project images and generated concept imagery are recorded in `docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md`. Label concept artwork where it is not an interface capture. Do not treat generated visuals as customer proof.

## Product Principles

- Keep existing functionality and project truth intact while improving presentation.
- Make service and package enquiries understandable without implying unapproved commercial promises.
- Preserve the user's chosen brand assets and distinguish artwork from the company identity.
- Record verification limits explicitly; build success alone does not demonstrate live service delivery.
