# Packages: decisions to discuss

The page intentionally publishes no amounts. Product types: simple website, portfolio website, e-commerce website, custom LMS, POS system, and AI-integrated website.

## Before publishing prices
1. Currency and primary market: LKR, USD, or both.
2. Typical scope: pages, products, users, branches, integrations, and content entry.
3. Full-project terms: deposit, milestones, acceptance, and handover.
4. Monthly model: financing a fixed build, subscription access, or ongoing development and care.
5. Minimum duration, setup fee, cancellation, and remaining payments.
6. Hosting, domain, backups, maintenance, and support coverage.
7. Source code ownership and third-party licensing.
8. AI usage charges and payment gateway transaction charges.
9. Tax treatment and change request pricing.

Suggested discussion order: define one simple website scope first, agree on its full-project cost structure, then decide what the monthly alternative includes. Use that model to price more complex categories. No amounts or binding terms are assumed in the current site.
